## 2025-02-23 - Dynamic status messages missing ARIA live regions
**Learning:** The application heavily relies on dynamic `<p class="surface-status">` tags to provide asynchronous feedback to users (e.g., action results, error messages, save statuses). However, these elements inherently lack `role="status"` or `aria-live` attributes, meaning screen readers do not proactively announce these critical updates, leading to a degraded experience for visually impaired users who cannot visually observe the status changes.
**Action:** When creating or updating dynamic status components (like `.surface-status`), always ensure they are accompanied by appropriate ARIA live regions: `role="status" aria-live="polite"` for general feedback and `role="alert" aria-live="assertive"` for errors or critical warnings. Consider a global approach or incorporating these attributes by default in future component designs.
